Conclusions carry no new evidence under `client_note.md` §3 — they restate, frame, or derive from

**(reuse)** where the cited annex row is tested first under another section, and the pass criterion is
presence/ordering rather than a new falsifier. Results and Discussion carry the annex's own falsifiers
directly.

## 5. What I expect to fail

D2, the ice-concentration credit paired with side A's own conditionality. It is the hardest test in
the plan to satisfy under drafting pressure: it requires two annex rows synthesized into one paragraph
that states a real credit and immediately qualifies it, rather than either (a) a bare "the report flags
this" sentence with no consequence drawn, which reads as a token credit and fails the pairing
requirement, or (b) the qualification swallowing the credit entirely, which fails `client_note.md` §7's
rule against declining to rank. This is also the exact imbalance The Manager's open §5 named as the
run's specific risk — an annex that reads six-axes-contested-and-three-credits-folded-in on the page
producing a note with the same lopsided shape even though the underlying content supports both halves.
D1 and D3 carry the same risk in a milder form; D2 is where the risk is sharpest because it is the only
credit test that requires a second row to complete it.
